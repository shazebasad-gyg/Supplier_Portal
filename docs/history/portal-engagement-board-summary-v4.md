# Supplier Portal Engagement — Board Summary
**Prepared:** April 9, 2026 | **Audience:** Director of Product → Board

---

## Executive Summary

The supplier portal is deeply embedded in the workflows of GYG's commercially active supplier base. Among the **46,218 suppliers who have transacted in the last 180 days**, **80.6% logged in within the last 30 days and 91.6% within the last 90 days**. The board-level signal is stronger: **97.4% of total platform GMV in any given month is generated by suppliers who logged in that month** — a rate stable between 94.8% and 96.4% for all 12 trailing months.

Absolute portal reach is expanding. **52,734 suppliers logged in during March 2026, up +26% year-on-year** (from 41,786 in April 2025), reaching a 12-month record.

Engagement scales almost perfectly with commercial weight. The top 1% of suppliers by GMV — **505 accounts generating €1.96B (42.8% of platform GMV)** — log in at 99.2% monthly. The top 10% (~5,000 suppliers, 84% of GMV) log in at 97%+. The portal has near-universal adoption among the suppliers that drive GYG's revenue.

The primary product investment opportunity sits at the entry level: **5,240 suppliers in the 1–13 bookings/year band** — operators who have demonstrated they can generate transactions on GYG but have not yet formed a consistent portal habit. Engaged peers in the same booking tier earn **34% more net revenue** (€384 vs. €287 average NR). Closing this habit gap is the highest-leverage activation investment available.

The recommended board-level KPI is **GMV-weighted engagement rate (L30)** — the share of monthly GMV generated by logged-in suppliers — because it is immune to denominator inflation from registry growth and declines only when the suppliers that matter most disengage.

## Key Insights

> **A board member can read these six insights and understand the full story.**

1. **97.4% of platform GMV is generated by suppliers who logged in that month.** This rate has been stable between 94.8% and 96.4% for every month of the trailing 12 months. There is no engagement problem with the suppliers who drive GYG's revenue.

2. **4 in 5 transacting suppliers visit the portal monthly; 9 in 10 quarterly.** Among the 46,218 suppliers with ≥1 booking in the last 180 days: 62.8% logged in within the last 7 days, **80.6% within the last 30 days**, and 91.6% within the last 90 days.

3. **Portal reach grew +26% year-on-year.** 52,734 suppliers logged in during March 2026 vs. 41,786 in April 2025, a 12-month record. Growth is accelerating into the peak booking season (Q1–Q2).

4. **Engagement and GMV are nearly perfectly correlated.** Top 1% of suppliers by GMV (505 accounts, €1.96B, 42.8% of total) log in at 99.2% monthly. Even the lowest GMV tier (€1–€948/year) logs in at 62.1% monthly — a meaningful engagement floor across all transacting operators.

5. **Connectivity is the most cost-efficient engagement lever.** Non-managed, non-connected suppliers — **85% of the base, logging in at 22.0% monthly** — jump to 57.0% L30 when connected (2.6× uplift) and to 84.0% when both connected and managed. Connectivity is supplier-initiated and requires no headcount scaling; it is associated with a **2.8× net revenue increase** (€3.5k → €9.8k average NR).

6. **5,240 entry-level suppliers are transacting but not in portal habit.** These suppliers in the 1–13 bookings/year band have proven they can generate bookings on GYG. Engaged peers in the same tier earn €384 vs. €287 average NR — a **34% premium**. Closing this gap is the highest-leverage product investment available.

## Key Metrics Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Active supplier** | A supplier on GYG's marketplace (`gyg_status = 'active'`, `supplier_type = 'Marketplace'`) with at least 1 non-fraud, non-zero-GMV booking in the last 180 days. This is the primary denominator for engagement analysis. **Current count: 46,218 suppliers.** |
| **L7 / L30 / L90** | At least one portal login in the last 7 / 30 / 90 calendar days. Derived from `last_login_date` in `dim_supplier_summary`. Presented as a % of the active supplier base unless otherwise stated. |
| **GMV-weighted login rate** | The share of total monthly platform GMV generated by suppliers who logged into the portal at least once that month. Calculated by joining monthly booking GMV to monthly login flags. This is the primary board KPI — it is immune to denominator inflation and declines only when high-GMV suppliers disengage. |
| **Absolute MAU** | Unique suppliers with at least one portal login in a calendar month. The reach metric — shows how many operators the portal touches each month, independent of denominator effects. |
| **Why 180 days?** | GYG's supplier base includes many seasonal operators (e.g., ski guides, summer tour operators) who may not generate bookings in any given 365-day window but are commercially active in 6-month seasons. A 180-day booking window captures these suppliers correctly and avoids miscategorising them as inactive. Using a 365-day window would exclude ~168 confirmed seasonal operators who transact regularly within their season. |
| **GMV** | Gross Merchandise Value in EUR — the total amount paid by customers at checkout, converted to EUR. Excludes fraudulent transactions and zero-value bookings. |
| **Connected** | Supplier uses a channel manager or API connectivity integration (`is_connected = 'Connected'` in `dim_supplier_summary`). Allows automated availability and booking synchronisation. |
| **Managed** | Supplier has a dedicated GYG account manager (`is_managed = 'Managed'` in `dim_supplier_summary`). |

**Key findings:**
- Engaged suppliers earn materially more NR than non-engaged suppliers **within the same booking tier** — the NR gap is not simply explained by busier suppliers logging in more

Causality requires a controlled experiment; feature-level engagement data (planned end of April 2026) will enable scoping one.

### Q9: Who are the non-engaging suppliers and what is the right response?

The 145,044 suppliers outside the ≥1 booking L180 cohort are not the portal's target audience — but they represent distinct strategic situations requiring different responses.

**Table 9.1 — Non-Transacting Supplier Cohorts**
*As of April 9, 2026 | Login rates for cohorts outside ≥1 booking L180*

| Cohort | Suppliers | % of Non-L180 Group | Avg Tenure | L30 Login Rate | L365 Login Rate | Avg Lifetime Bookings |
|--------|-----------|---------------------|------------|---------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Never transacted (lifetime) | 113,037 | **78.0%** | 2.1 years | 4.4%  | 42.7% | 0   |
| New (registered ≤90 days)   | 16,830  | 11.6%   | <0.3 years | **57.1%** | 98.1% | 1   |
| Churned (gone dark)         | 8,115   | 5.6%    | 6.0 years  | **0.0%**  | 0.0%  | 198 |
| Dormant (active but no recent bookings) | 3,254 | 2.2% | 3.9 years | 18.0% | 100.0% | 276 |

*Note: L7 and L90 breakdowns for each cohort, and year-on-year comparison of cohort sizes (to assess whether the Churned cohort is growing), require additional queries.*

**Group 1 — Never transacted (113,037 suppliers, 78% of non-L180 group)**
These suppliers have been on the platform for an average of **2.1 years without a single booking**. 57% have not logged in at all in the last 12 months. This is structural marketplace composition — suppliers who registered speculatively, cannot compete in their destination, or operate in low-demand categories. Portal investment will not change their outcome.
**Recommended response:** Passive maintenance. Automated listing quality requirements and nurture sequences only; no AM or product resource allocation.

**Group 2 — New suppliers (16,830 suppliers, <90 days registered)**
The highest-engagement group outside the transacting cohort: **57.1% L30**, with 98.1% logging in at least once in their registration period. Average 1 lifetime booking, actively onboarding. The registration-to-first-booking window is the critical habit formation moment.
**Recommended response:** Invest in the onboarding activation funnel. Identify the specific portal actions most correlated with time-to-second-booking (availability setup, first inquiry response, pricing configuration) and build guided activation flows around those moments.

**Group 3 — Churned (8,115 suppliers, avg 198 lifetime bookings, 6-year tenure)**
Former high-performers who have gone completely silent: **0.0% login at any window**. These suppliers have almost certainly migrated to competing platforms or exited the market. Portal-led interventions cannot reach them.
**Recommended response:** AM-led win-back outreach (email/phone) where NR history justifies the investment cost. Prioritise the highest-NR accounts in this cohort.

**Group 4 — Dormant (3,254 suppliers, avg 276 lifetime bookings, 4-year tenure)**
High-performers who still visit the portal (**18.0% L30, 100.0% L365**) but have received no bookings in the last year. They are engaged but facing a commercial problem — likely ranking deterioration, availability gaps, or seasonal trough. The portal habit is intact; the booking problem requires commercial intervention.
**Recommended response:** Proactive content quality and availability insights surfaced through the portal; flag to AM for review where applicable. This cohort represents €0 current NR but has the highest historical performance of any non-transacting group.

### Q10: What should GYG's north star metrics for portal engagement be?

**Table 10.1 — Recommended Metric Framework**

| Level | Metric | Definition | Current Value | Target |
|-------|--------|-----------|--------------|--------|
| **Board (primary)** | GMV-weighted login rate (L30) | % of total monthly platform GMV generated by suppliers with ≥1 login in the last 30 days | **97.4%** | ≥95.0% floor |
| **Board (secondary)** | Absolute MAU | Unique suppliers with ≥1 login in the calendar month | **52,734** (March 2026) | Growing YoY |
| **Operational (product)** | Engaged supplier rate L30 (≥1 booking L180) | L30 login rate among suppliers with ≥1 booking in the prior 180 days | **80.6%** | Trending toward 85%+ |

**Why GMV-weighted login rate is the right board KPI:**
- Immune to denominator inflation from supplier registry growth
- Declines only when high-GMV suppliers disengage — the actual risk worth monitoring
- Does not create perverse incentives around supplier base composition (adding low-GMV suppliers doesn't dilute the metric)
- Directly ties portal engagement to commercial outcomes

**Why absolute MAU complements it:**
- Shows platform reach expansion without denominator distortion
- Grows in absolute terms even as the registry expands
- Provides context for the GMV-weighted rate (breadth vs. depth)

**What to watch:**
- **GMV-weighted rate dropping below 95%** would warrant immediate investigation — it would mean high-GMV suppliers are disengaging from the portal
- **Absolute MAU growth decelerating** would indicate the platform is losing reach among newly onboarding suppliers
- **L30 rate for ≥1 booking L180 suppliers declining** would indicate a product experience or notification problem within the active base

## Data Sources & Methodology

### Tables Used

| Table | Catalog / Schema | Description |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| `production.supply_analytics.dim_supplier_summary` | production.supply_analytics | Supplier master — one row per supplier. Includes lifetime and rolling login, booking, and NR metrics. |
| `production.supply_analytics.fact_supplier_history` | production.supply_analytics | Daily supplier snapshot — one row per supplier per date, partitioned by `date`. Used for MAU trend and login frequency. |
| `production.dwh.fact_booking` | production.dwh | Booking-level fact table. Used to compute GMV L365 and L180 bookings per supplier. |

### Filters Applied

| Filter | Value | Reason |
|--------|-------|--------|
| `gyg_status = 'active'` | Active only | Excludes deleted / deactivated suppliers |
| `supplier_type = 'Marketplace'` | Marketplace only | Excludes T&T (GYG own-inventory) — not portal-relevant |
| `is_fraud = FALSE` | fact_booking only | Excludes fraudulent transactions from all GMV calculations |
| `gmv > 0` | fact_booking only | Excludes cancelled / zero-value bookings |

### Primary Denominator

**Active supplier (≥1 booking L180):** Any supplier matching the above filters with at least one non-fraud, non-zero-GMV booking in `fact_booking` where `date_of_checkout >= CURRENT_DATE - 180`. Yields **46,218 suppliers** as of April 9, 2026.

### Data Gaps and Planned Improvements

- **Login is the only engagement signal currently available.** Feature usage, page views, and time-on-site are planned for end of April 2026. A supplier who logs in once counts equally to one who spends hours in pricing tools. Richer data will enable depth vs. breadth analysis.
- **L7 and L90 breakdowns for segments, managed/connected tiers, and non-transacting cohorts** are not included in this document. They are derivable from `fact_supplier_history` with additional queries.
- **Year-on-year comparison of cohort sizes** (e.g., whether the Churned cohort is growing) requires a second snapshot query from 12 months prior.
- **Login frequency by GMV tier** (e.g., what share of 10+ login/month suppliers are in each GMV band) requires joining `fact_supplier_history` frequency data to GMV bands — not yet run.
- **API-connected operator flag:** The 9.3k+ bookings tier shows slightly lower L30 in some filters, likely large API-integrated operators who transact without portal UI. Once an API-transaction flag is available, this segment should be reported separately.
