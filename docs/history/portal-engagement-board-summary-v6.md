# Supplier Portal Engagement — Board Summary
**Audience:** Director of Product → Board

---

## Section 1: Key Insights

1. **97.4%** of monthly platform GMV is generated by suppliers who logged into the portal that month; over the past 12 months this rate ranged from **94.8%** to **96.4%**, with March 2026 reaching **97.4%**.
2. Among the **46,218** commercially active suppliers (≥1 booking in the last 180 days), **62.8%** logged in the last 7 days, **80.6%** in the last 30 days, and **91.6%** in the last 90 days.
3. Portal reach grew **+26%** year-on-year — **52,734** unique supplier logins in March 2026 vs. **41,786** in April 2025, a 12-month record.
4. Connected suppliers log in at **2.6×** the rate of non-connected suppliers: non-managed, non-connected suppliers (**85%** of the base) log in at **21.7%** L30; non-managed, connected suppliers log in at **56.9%** L30, associated with a **2.8×** average NR difference (€3.5k → €9.8k).
5. The churned cohort — suppliers who previously had bookings but dropped to zero bookings and zero logins in the last 365 days — grew **+48.7%** year-on-year, from **5,462** to **8,125** operators averaging **198** lifetime bookings and **6 years** of platform tenure.
6. **5,240** suppliers in the 1–13 bookings/year band are transacting but not visiting the portal monthly; engaged peers in the same booking tier earn **34%** more net revenue (**€384** vs. **€287** average NR).

## Section 2: Detailed Analysis

---

**Q1: How many suppliers are actively using the portal?**

The commercially relevant supplier universe is the **46,218** operators who have generated at least one booking in the last 180 days. Within this group, **62.8%** logged in within the last 7 days, **80.6%** within the last 30 days, and **91.6%** within the last 90 days. A further **168** suppliers have ≥1 booking L180 but zero in the last 365 days — confirmed seasonal operators who log in at **54.8%** L30. The total registered active marketplace base is **191,262** suppliers, but **145,044** have no booking in the last 180 days and using them as a denominator produces a distorted rate of **27.7%** L30 that declines mechanically as GYG registers more suppliers.

- **168** confirmed seasonal operators (≥1 booking L180, 0 bookings L365) engage at **54.8%** L30, higher than any inactive cohort in the registry

**Q3: How frequently do suppliers log in?**

Among suppliers who do log in, the distribution is bimodal: **35.2%** are power users averaging 10+ logins per month, while **30.4%** log in only 1–2 times per month. This bimodal pattern is almost entirely explained by GMV tier — **90.5%** of €1.5M+ suppliers are power users, compared to just **5.5%** of entry-tier (€1–€948) suppliers. The portal is already a daily operational tool for high-value suppliers; the product challenge is driving that same depth of use further down the GMV distribution.

**Q4: Which supplier segments are most and least engaged?**

Scale Seekers and Leisure Brands log in at roughly **3×** the rate of Independent Creators at L30 (**61.0–61.5%** vs. **20.3%**). The engagement gap is consistent across all three windows — Scale Seekers at **47.4%** L7 vs. Independent Creators at **11.6%** — and widens at shorter windows, suggesting Independent Creators visit the portal for specific periodic tasks rather than regular operational use. The "Unclear" segment (**31,175** suppliers at **6.9%** L30) consists primarily of newly registered or unclassified accounts.

**Q5: Does account management or connectivity change engagement?**

Both account management and connectivity are independently associated with higher login rates, and the combination produces the highest engagement of any group. Non-managed, non-connected suppliers — **85%** of the total base — log in at just **21.7%** L30 and **13.6%** L7. Non-managed, connected suppliers log in at **56.9%** L30, associated with a **2.8×** average NR difference vs. non-connected peers (€3.5k → €9.8k). The managed + connected combination reaches **83.9%** L30, the highest of any group, with an average NR of **€161k** per supplier.

**Q8: Does portal engagement correlate with commercial performance?**

Engaged suppliers earn materially more net revenue than non-engaged suppliers within the same booking band, meaning the NR gap is not simply explained by higher-volume suppliers having more reason to log in. The within-tier NR premium ranges from **1.3×** at the entry level (1–13 bookings) to **3.2×** at the top tier (759+ bookings). At the 1–13 bookings level, engaged suppliers earn **€384** vs. **€287** per year — a **€97 premium** that across **11,088** engaged suppliers in this band represents approximately **€1.1M** in additional aggregate NR.

**Q9: Who are the non-engaging suppliers?**

The **145,044** suppliers outside the ≥1 booking L180 cohort split into four groups with distinct characteristics and different strategic responses required. The largest group — **113,037** suppliers who have never generated a single booking — have been on the platform an average of **2.1 years** and represent structural marketplace composition, not an engagement failure. The churned cohort grew **+48.7%** year-on-year (5,462 → **8,125** former operators) and has gone completely silent — **0.0%** login rate at any window — making them unreachable through the portal.

- New suppliers (<90 days registered) log in at **57.1%** L30 and **98.1%** L365, making this the highest-engagement non-transacting group — the registration-to-first-booking window is the critical activation moment
- Dormant suppliers (**3,254** operators, avg **276** lifetime bookings) still visit the portal (**18.0%** L30, **100%** L365) but have received no bookings in the last year — their portal habit is intact but they face a commercial problem, not a portal problem

**Q10: What metrics should track portal engagement?**

Three metrics together give a complete picture of portal health. The GMV-weighted login rate is the primary board KPI because it is immune to denominator inflation from registry growth and declines only when commercially important suppliers disengage — a sustained drop below **95%** would be the signal to act on. Absolute MAU measures platform breadth without distortion and shows whether the portal is reaching more operators each year. The L30 rate within the ≥1 booking L180 base measures product performance against the operators the portal is designed to serve.

**Key findings:**
- **GMV-weighted login rate (L30): 97.4%** — current value as of March 2026; target floor **≥95.0%**; ranged from **94.8%** to **96.4%** across the prior 11 months
- **Absolute MAU: 52,734** (March 2026) — current value; target: growing year-on-year; **+26%** vs. April 2025
- **L30 rate, ≥1 booking L180: 80.6%** — current value; target: trending toward **85%+**; activation sub-metric: 1–13 bookings band currently at **57.5%**, target **70%+**

## Section 3: Appendix

### Key Metrics Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Active supplier** | Marketplace supplier (`gyg_status = 'active'`, `supplier_type = 'Marketplace'`) with ≥1 non-fraud, non-zero-GMV booking in the last 180 days. Primary denominator: **46,218 suppliers** as of April 9, 2026. |
| **L7 / L30 / L90** | At least one portal login in the last 7 / 30 / 90 calendar days, derived from `last_login_date` in `dim_supplier_summary`. |
| **GMV-weighted login rate** | % of total monthly platform GMV generated by suppliers who logged in at least once that month. Calculated by joining monthly booking GMV to monthly login flags in `fact_supplier_history`. |
| **Absolute MAU** | Unique suppliers with ≥1 login in a calendar month. Unaffected by registry composition changes. |
| **Why 180 days?** | Seasonal operators transact in 6-month windows and would be incorrectly excluded by a 365-day booking filter. The 180-day window captures **168** confirmed seasonal operators who are active but not visible in an annual cut. |
| **GMV** | Gross Merchandise Value in EUR at checkout. Excludes fraud (`is_fraud = FALSE`) and zero-value bookings (`gmv > 0`). |
| **Connected** | Supplier uses a channel manager or API connectivity integration (`is_connected = 'Connected'`). |
| **Managed** | Supplier has a dedicated GYG account manager (`is_managed = 'Managed'`). |
| **Churned** | Lifetime bookings > 0, zero bookings L365, zero logins L365. |
| **Dormant** | Lifetime bookings > 0, zero bookings L365, ≥1 login L365. |
