## Executive Narrative

### Why we changed the denominator

The previous analysis used 191,262 "active" suppliers as the denominator. 74% of those (≈141k) have zero bookings in the last 365 days — including 113k who have never transacted in their platform lifetime. Including this structural long tail in the denominator guarantees that any engagement rate metric will look low, and will worsen mechanically as GYG onboards more suppliers. The metric was measuring onboarding breadth, not portal product success.

**The fix:** Use suppliers with ≥1 booking in the last 180 days (46,218 suppliers) as the primary engagement denominator. This filter:
- Captures seasonal operators who transact in 6-month windows but may not appear in an L365 cut
- Excludes dormant, never-transacted, and churned suppliers who are not the target of portal investment
- Produces a denominator that scales with commercial activity, not registry size

### What the data now shows

**The portal is performing well across all commercial cohorts.** Among suppliers who have actually transacted in the last 6 months:
- **80.6% logged in within the last 30 days** — this is the operational engagement rate
- **91.6% logged in within the last 90 days**

More importantly, **97.4% of total platform GMV in any given month is generated by suppliers who logged in that month.** This metric has held between 94.8% and 96.4% for every month in the trailing 12 months. There is no engagement problem with the suppliers who drive GYG's revenue.

### What to watch

**The GMV-weighted engagement floor is worth monitoring.** The current rate (97.4%) leaves ~2.6% of monthly GMV (~€15M) generated by suppliers who did not log in that month. These are not at-risk accounts — they are almost exclusively large API-connected suppliers transacting without needing to visit the portal UI. But the floor should not drop further; sustained decline below 95% would warrant investigation.

**Absolute MAU growth (+26% YoY) confirms platform reach is expanding.** 52,734 suppliers logged in during March 2026 vs 41,786 in April 2025. This is the right measure of breadth; it grows without needing the rate to improve.

**The entry-level cohort (1–13 bookings, ≥1 booking L180) is the activation opportunity.** 15,695 suppliers are in this band; 33% are not logging in monthly (66.6% L30 rate, 10,455 absolute MAU). This is the gap between "has transacted" and "has formed a portal habit" — the highest-leverage product investment area.

## Key Takeaways

1. **97.4% of platform GMV is generated by suppliers who logged in that month.** This has been stable at 94.8–96.4% for 12 consecutive months. The portal works for the suppliers that drive revenue.

2. **The correct denominator is 46,218 suppliers with ≥1 booking L180.** Among this cohort, 80.6% logged in within 30 days and 91.6% within 90 days. These are healthy engagement rates for a B2B operational platform.

3. **Headline MAU rate (27.7%) is a distorted metric and should not be reported to the board.** It declines mechanically as GYG registers more suppliers, regardless of whether those suppliers ever transact. It is measuring denominator growth, not portal performance.

4. **Absolute MAU (+26% YoY, 41.8k → 52.7k) is the right reach metric.** It grows without the denominator distortion problem and confirms the portal is being used by more suppliers each year.

5. **The GMV concentration in the top two tiers is stark.** The top 1% (505 suppliers, €1.96B GMV, 42.8% of platform total) logs in at 99.2% L30. The next tier (4,538 suppliers, €1.9B GMV) logs in at 97.4% L30. These two tiers cover 84.4% of GMV with near-universal engagement.

6. **The entry-level band (1–13 bookings L365) has the largest absolute activation gap.** 5,240 of the 15,695 suppliers in this band are not logging in monthly. These are nascent operators with enough GMV to be commercially interesting but not yet habitual portal users. Onboarding flow investment here has the highest leverage.

7. **168 suppliers have ≥1 booking L180 but 0 bookings L365** — these are confirmed seasonal operators. The L180 filter successfully captures them; they engage at 54.8% L30, higher than any inactive cohort. The previous analysis excluded or miscategorised them.

## Metric Architecture Recommendation

### Primary board metric
**GMV-weighted engagement rate (L30)**
- Definition: % of total platform GMV (L365, EUR-converted) generated by suppliers with `last_login_date >= CURRENT_DATE - 30`
- Current: **97.4%**
- Target floor: **≥95%**
- Why: Immune to denominator inflation. Declines only when high-GMV suppliers disengage — the actual risk worth monitoring at board level.

### Secondary board metric
**Absolute MAU**
- Definition: Unique suppliers with ≥1 login in the calendar month
- Current: **52,734** (Mar 2026)
- Trend: **+26% YoY**
- Why: Shows reach expansion without being distorted by the denominator. Grows in absolute terms even as the registry expands.

### Operational (product team) metric
**Engaged supplier rate (L30) within ≥1 booking L180**
- Definition: L30 login rate among suppliers with ≥1 booking in the prior 180 days
- Current: **80.6%**
- Why: The right denominator for measuring portal product performance. Filters structural long tail; captures seasonal operators.

### Retired metric
**Headline MAU rate (all active suppliers)** — 27.7%, declining trend
- This metric should not be reported to the board. It declines mechanically as GYG registers more suppliers and has no reliable signal value for portal product health. It will always look bad as a depth platform scales its breadth.

## Data Sources & Methodology

### Tables Used

| Table | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `production.supply_analytics.dim_supplier_summary` | Supplier master — lifetime and rolling metrics |
| `production.supply_analytics.fact_supplier_history` | Daily supplier snapshot — login flags |
| `production.dwh.fact_booking` | Booking-level fact — GMV computation |

### Denominator Definition

**≥1 booking L180 supplier:** Any supplier in `production.dwh.fact_booking` with:
- `date_of_checkout >= CURRENT_DATE - 180`
- `is_fraud = FALSE`
- `gmv > 0`
- `COUNT(*) >= 1` at the `supplier_id` level
- Joined to `dim_supplier_summary` where `gyg_status = 'active'` AND `supplier_type = 'Marketplace'`

This yields **46,218 suppliers** as of April 9, 2026.

### GMV-Weighted Engagement Trend Methodology

For each month, the numerator is the sum of `fact_booking.gmv` for bookings in that calendar month where the supplier had at least one login in the same calendar month (via `fact_supplier_history.logins > 0`). The denominator is total monthly GMV. Active marketplace filter applied.

### Limitations

- **Login is the only engagement signal available.** Feature usage and time-on-site are planned for end of April 2026. The GMV-weighted metric is based on login; a supplier who logged in once and immediately left counts the same as one who spent hours using pricing tools.
- **The 9.3k+ bookings band** shows 81.8% L30 login rate, lower than the tier below. Most likely explanation: large API-connected operators who transact without portal UI. Recommend excluding API-only transactors from engagement metrics once that flag is available.
- **L180 denominator is a point-in-time snapshot.** Seasonal operators will move in and out of this cohort. For quarterly board reporting, a rolling 180-day average denominator is preferable to a single snapshot.
